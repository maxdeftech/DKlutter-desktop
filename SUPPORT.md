# A little help, when you need it

For help with DKlutter, email **[maxwellinnovativetechnologies@gmail.com](mailto:maxwellinnovativetechnologies@gmail.com)**.

You can also [report a Mac app problem](https://github.com/maxdeftech/DKlutter-desktop/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).

Please include:

- Your DKlutter version, shown in **DKlutter → About DKlutter**.
- Your macOS version and whether your Mac uses Apple Silicon or Intel.
- What you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected.
- The wording of any error message. Screenshots can help after you hide private information.

Never post passwords, sign-in links, access codes, payment details or private workspace content in a public issue.

## Before reporting a problem

**Installing an update:** quit DKlutter, download the current installer, and replace the app in Applications. Launch the copy in Applications.

**Google sign-in:** finish in your browser, then allow it to open DKlutter when asked. Keep the app open while signing in.

**Notifications:** allow DKlutter in System Settings → Notifications, enable notifications inside DKlutter, and check whether Focus is silencing alerts. Notifications pause when you quit the app completely.

**Trouble opening the app:** tell us the exact macOS message you see. Keep your Mac’s security settings enabled.

For a private account or billing question, use email rather than a public issue.
