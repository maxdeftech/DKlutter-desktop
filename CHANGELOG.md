# What’s new in DKlutter

A plain-language history of workspace features and Mac app improvements. Only the current installer is available here.

## 1.1.1 · November 18, 2026

### Mac app & installer

- Google sign-in now opens in your usual browser and brings you back to DKlutter after you sign in.
- Improved sign-in for a fresh installation on another Mac.

## 1.1.0 · November 18, 2026

### Features

- Added Mac notification delivery for workspace alerts, with controls in DKlutter’s notification settings.

### Mac app & installer

- Notifications can continue when you close the app window; reopening brings you back to your workspace.
- Notification clicks take you to the relevant area of DKlutter.
- Added an option to launch DKlutter when you log in to your Mac.

## 1.0.2 · November 17, 2026

### Mac app & installer

- Improved how sign-in windows and account redirects were handled. This was followed by the browser-based Google sign-in update in 1.1.1.

## 1.0.1 · November 17, 2026

### Mac app & installer

- Fixed a startup issue that could leave the app window blank while waiting for fonts to load.

## 1.0.0 · November 17, 2026

### Features

- Brought the DKlutter planning workspace to macOS, including tasks, notes, calendars, boards and timetables.

### Mac app & installer

- Introduced the Mac app, Dock access, printing and PDF export.

## Recent workspace improvements

- Clarity brings planning suggestions and workspace help into a more focused conversation.
- Personal colours and light or dark appearance help make your workspace feel like yours.
- Notes support easier table removal and spelling corrections that stay saved.
- Notification links open the relevant task, note, conversation or announcement.
